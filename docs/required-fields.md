# Health insurance claim form – required fields

Scope: Finnish health insurance context. Exact mandatory fields vary by insurer and policy. The following is a proposed prototype form based on Finnish insurer guidance and Kela's medical-expense claim form.

These fields are planned for a later development stage. They are not implemented in the current five-stage journey placeholders.

## Step 1 – Claim and insurance information

- Claim type (illness / accident)
- Insurance policy number
- Coverage number (if applicable)
- Date of incident or symptom onset
- Country where incident/treatment occurred

## Step 2 – Claimant information

- First name
- Last name
- Finnish personal identity code
- Date of birth (if personal identity code unavailable)
- Phone number
- Email address
- Relationship to insured person (self / child / other)

## Step 3 – Incident / illness description

- Type of incident (illness / accident)
- Date symptoms started or accident occurred
- Description of illness or accident
- What happened?
- Where did it happen?
- Was another party involved? (if applicable)
- Was it work-related or traffic-related? (if applicable)
- Name of other insurance company (if applicable)

## Step 4 – Medical treatment information

- Healthcare provider / clinic name
- Treatment location
- Date of medical appointment
- Doctor's name (if available)
- Type of treatment received
- Diagnosis or reason for treatment
- Referral details (if applicable)

## Step 5 – Medical expenses

- Expense category (consultation / examination / treatment / medication / other)
- Date of expense
- Description of expense
- Total amount paid
- Currency
- Amount reimbursed by Kela or another provider (if applicable)
- Remaining amount claimed

## Step 6 – Supporting documents

- Medical certificate or doctor's statement
- Medical referral (if applicable)
- Itemised invoice
- Payment receipt
- Prescription (if applicable)
- Pharmacy receipt (if applicable)
- Other supporting documents

## Step 7 – Reimbursement details

- Bank account holder's name
- IBAN
- BIC (if foreign bank account)

## Step 8 – Review and declaration

- Review all submitted information
- Confirm information is correct
- Claimant's name
- Submission date
- Declaration of accuracy
- Electronic signature / confirmation
